# Jordansky

**Tax Consultant & Machine Learning Engineer — Amsterdam, Netherlands**

I work across two disciplines that rarely meet: tax advisory and applied
machine learning. The first taught me to read rules carefully and defend a
position with evidence. The second taught me that most systems fail for
reasons nobody wrote down.

---

### Tax Advisory

Corporate and cross-border tax structuring, compliance review, and transfer
pricing documentation. I advise on restructuring decisions before they become
filings, and translate obligations into something a finance team can act on.

### Machine Learning

Fine-tuning and evaluation of generative models — LoRA adaptation, adaptive
hyperparameter selection, and reproducible training pipelines. Currently
competing in decentralised model-training networks, where results are scored
against a validator rather than a benchmark I chose myself.
